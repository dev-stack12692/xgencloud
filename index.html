Common questions

The short answers before you order.

How do I order?

DM us on Discord with the plan you want. This preview site has no live ordering - accounts and online payments are not available yet.

Do I get a public IP?

Yes, as an add-on. Shared IPv4 (₹65/mo) gives port forwarding and IP + port access - great for Minecraft and game servers. Dedicated public IPv4 (₹265/mo) is your own public address for websites, APIs and apps.

Can I run a Minecraft server?

Yes. VPS plans are Minecraft and Pterodactyl friendly, and every Minecraft server gets a free servername.xgencloud.bond subdomain.

What does bot hosting include?

Shared-RAM plans from ₹30/month for hosting your Discord bot - the starter plan has 360MB RAM, 30% shared CPU and 1GB disk.

How do I contact support?

On Discord, or by email at support@xgencloud.bond.

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final - every plan is no-refunds.
